Vehicles, Campers & Components
  • Opportunities span complete vehicles, camper units, and modular boxes — not only accessories
  • Plus certified components: refrigeration, HVAC, electrical/solar systems, chassis technology, and modular windows
Process & Know-How Gap
  • Beyond hardware — local builders need production processes, quality standardization, industrialization methods, and the technical training to apply them
Regulatory Tailwinds
  • Trade opening in Argentina and mandatory homologation requirements in both nations are pushing coachbuilders toward certified international parts
